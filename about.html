Our story

Green Valley Farm is dedicated to quality agricultural production, sustainable practices and sharing knowledge that helps farming communities thrive. We believe farms can create opportunity while protecting the natural resources that future generations depend on.

Our work is guided by integrity, continuous learning, quality and respect for the environment.

Our mission

To produce quality agricultural products and promote responsible farming practices.

To be a trusted example of productive, sustainable and community-minded farming.
