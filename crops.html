Crop Farming

We grow a range of nutritious crops using practical, soil-conscious methods and careful farm management.

Crops we focus on

Crop selection depends on soil, rainfall, season and market demand. These are examples of crops that can form part of a productive Ugandan farm.

Maize

A staple crop that supports households, livestock and local markets.

Vegetables

Fresh seasonal vegetables grown with attention to soil and water.

Coffee

A valuable cash crop with careful growing and post-harvest handling.

Our growing approach

  • Prepare and improve the soil
  • Choose suitable seed varieties
  • Manage weeds, pests and diseases responsibly
  • Harvest and store produce carefully
